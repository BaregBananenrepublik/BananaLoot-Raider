BananaLoot Raider

A lightweight companion addon for Vanilla WoW raiders using BananaLoot.

BananaLoot Raider enhances the raider-side loot experience on OctoWoW and Turtle WoW (Vanilla 1.12) with convenient roll synchronization, shared loot history, and easy Soft Reserve communication.

It is designed to work alongside BananaLoot without replacing or interfering with the loot master's responsibilities.

Features

- Roll Sync Popup: Receive live loot roll broadcasts from a BananaLoot loot master. View item details, the countdown, and incoming rolls, and roll Main Spec (MS), Off Spec (OS), or Transmog (TM) directly from the popup.
- Round Robin Support: Displays Round Robin priorities and highlights rolls that are no longer eligible.
- Shared Loot History: View items awarded during the current session, including the winner and loot category.
- Quick Soft Reserve Communication: Send "sr [Item]" and "unsr [Item]" whispers to your loot master using Shift-click item links.
- Optional Trade Tracking: Detect and broadcast trades involving items awarded through BananaLoot, helping raids keep track of item transfers.
- Customizable Interface: German and English localization, adjustable window scaling, sound options, and a movable minimap button.
- Item Database: Includes a static item database for displaying item names, quality colors, and icons even if your client has never encountered the item before.

Important

BananaLoot Raider is not a standalone loot system.

It does not manage Soft Reserves, maintain the loot master's reservation lists, or award items. All loot management and distribution remain the responsibility of BananaLoot and the raid's loot master.

Installation

1. Download the latest release.
2. Extract the "BananaLootRaider" folder into your "World of Warcraft/Interface/AddOns/" directory.
3. Restart WoW or reload your UI.
4. Enable BananaLoot Raider in the AddOns menu.

Slash Commands

- "/blr" – Open or close the shared loot history.
- "/blr sr" – Open the Soft Reserve communication window.
- "/blr options" – Open the addon settings.
- "/blr help" – Show available commands.

BananaLoot Raider is an independent community addon developed for the BananaLoot ecosystem. It is a work in progress, and feedback and contributions are welcome.
